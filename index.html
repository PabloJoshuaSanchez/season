Load your league from ESPN

1. Enter your league ID — it's the leagueId= number in your ESPN league URL.

2. A page of raw data opens. You're already logged in to ESPN, so it shows your private league. Select all, copy.

3. Paste it here and press Load.

Takes under a minute. Do it after waivers clear each week, or any time you want fresh rosters. Easier on a laptop than a phone.

Or paste the draft app CSV instead (pre-draft rosters only)
